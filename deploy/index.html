About GEMCA

Built on integrity. Defined by outcomes.

GEMCA — Goraya Education & Migration Consultant Australia — was founded on a simple conviction: that navigating Australia's migration system should feel less like a gamble and more like a strategy.

Led by registered migration agent Ansar Goraya, we combine deep regulatory knowledge with a genuinely personal approach. Every client is assessed individually, advised honestly, and represented with the diligence their future deserves.

From international students to skilled professionals, partners and business migrants — we translate complexity into clear, actionable pathways.
