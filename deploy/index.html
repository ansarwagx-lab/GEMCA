FAQ

Common Questions

The right visa depends on your occupation, qualifications, English proficiency, age, family situation, sponsorship availability, and intended state of residence. Our assessment covers all of these factors and maps them to the visa subclasses you're eligible for — including any pathways you may not have considered. Book a consultation to get a clear picture.

Processing times vary significantly by visa type. Partner visas currently take 12–28 months; skilled independent (189) can be 12–18 months after invitation; student visas are often processed within 4–8 weeks. Employer-sponsored visas (482) are typically 3–6 months. We provide realistic timelines specific to your visa and current Department processing queues at the outset of your engagement.

You can apply directly, but MARA registered agents provide important protections: accurate advice, professional responsibility, and accountability under a Code of Conduct. For complex applications — points-tested visas, partner visas, AAT appeals, or any case with complications — professional representation significantly reduces the risk of refusal or delay.

Required documents depend entirely on your visa type. Broadly, most applications involve identity documents (passport, birth certificate), qualifications, employment records, skills assessment outcomes, English language test results, health examinations, and police clearances. We provide you with a tailored document checklist and review everything before lodgement.

Our fees are transparent and agreed in a Client Agreement before we begin any work. They vary by visa type and complexity. Government visa application charges (paid directly to the Department of Home Affairs) are separate and will be clearly outlined. Contact us for a fee outline specific to your situation.

A refusal is not necessarily the end of your migration journey. Depending on the visa type and grounds for refusal, you may be able to appeal to the Administrative Appeals Tribunal (AAT), request Ministerial Intervention, or lodge a fresh application with strengthened evidence. We assist with all of these options and will advise honestly on the merits of each path.

Yes. All client information is handled in strict confidence under the MARA Code of Conduct and the Privacy Act 1988. We do not share your information with any third party without your explicit consent, except where required by law or the Department of Home Affairs in the course of your application.
